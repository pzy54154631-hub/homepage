About

I am an undergraduate student in Statistics and Data Science at the University of Colorado Boulder. I previously studied at Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University.

My interests lie in statistical learning, model evaluation, and reliable AI systems. I have worked on cross-sectional time-series modeling, uncertainty-aware visual reasoning, and constrained AI agents. I am interested in applying these methods to scientific data and learning new domain-specific research techniques.

I am open to undergraduate research opportunities and collaboration.

Selected work

2026

Equity Return Prediction and Low-Turnover Stock Selection

2026 Shenzhen International FinTech Competition (FinTechathon) · Data Analytics Track · Top 25 (top 10%), semifinal in progress

Cross-sectional next-day return prediction using daily price-volume data from 4,650 A-share stocks. The approach combines XGBoost and GRU signals with a separate, persistent stock-selection layer and daily ranking layer. Development-period validation reduced Top-group Jaccard turnover from approximately 0.76–0.80 to 0.02–0.04, with a trade-off in excess return.

2026

Ambiguity Is Not One Scalar

Visual question answering · uncertainty-aware inference

Explores when a model should answer, ask for clarification, or abstain. Explicit enumeration of plausible referents recovered 20 of 22 human-audited ambiguity misses in a diagnostic analysis; results are specific to the reported test setting.

PW-OFPC: Precision-Weighted Object-File Predictive Coding

Abstract visual reasoning · structured uncertainty

A research pipeline for representing uncertain object attributes and comparing multiple answer candidates. On public RAVEN, the reported inference ladder reached 0.857 macro accuracy against a 0.537 direct-prompting baseline, with component-level evaluations.

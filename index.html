About

I am an undergraduate student in Statistics and Data Science at the University of Colorado Boulder. My research explores how AI systems can reason reliably under uncertainty: identify ambiguous visual referents, compare structured alternatives, calibrate their confidence, and decide when to answer, clarify, or abstain.

Beyond visual reasoning, I build systems that separate model-generated proposals from independently verified decisions. I am particularly interested in trustworthy multimodal AI, mechanistic interpretability, AI systems, and computational neuroscience.

I welcome research collaborations in reliable reasoning, uncertainty-aware inference, interpretable AI, and computational models of intelligence.

Research · Visual Question Answering

Ambiguity Is Not One Scalar

An ambiguity-aware VQA router that explicitly enumerates plausible referents before choosing among ANSWER / CLARIFY / ABSTAIN. In a focused human-audited diagnostic, referent enumeration recovered 20/22 previously missed ambiguous cases, compared with 0/22 for answer self-consistency.

Research · Abstract Visual Reasoning

PW-OFPC: Precision-Weighted Object-File Predictive Coding

An object-centric reasoning method that retains uncertain object-file representations and compares candidate solutions using precision-weighted predictive-coding residuals. On the public RAVEN benchmark, the reported inference ladder improved macro accuracy from 0.537 to 0.857.
